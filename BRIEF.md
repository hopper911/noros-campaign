# Noros Application Add-on Campaign

**Independent portfolio work. Not commissioned by or affiliated with North.Cloud or Noros.**

## Concept

**Ask your cloud what it costs—and why.**

An independently initiated product-launch campaign that translates the Noros AI FinOps story for three buyers: CFO, FinOps leader, and Engineer. Purpose: demonstrate intentional interest, fast iteration, and the ability to adapt a technical product story to different audiences.

## Audiences

| Audience | Message emphasis |
|----------|------------------|
| CFO | Financial visibility, forecast confidence, savings and business risk |
| FinOps leader | Allocation, governance, anomaly detection and optimization workflow |
| Engineer | Fast answers, resource context and actionable recommendations |
